# Make the versioned Official Rubric the read-only scoring authority

Academic Writing Task 2 scoring consumers use `rubrics/task2/current.json` as the machine-readable entry point and retain `rubric_version` in every result, benchmark run, criterion card, and Synthetic Reference. The canonical source is the immutable version directory named by that entry point. Consumers may read but must not amend or extend official descriptors. Internal interpretations are explicitly marked `DERIVED_INTERNAL`; research, teacher, student, target-band, and synthetic data remain outside the authority layer. A future rubric update creates a new semantic version and triggers rubric validation plus scoring, generator, retrieval, and benchmark regressions. This establishes the authority contract now without refactoring the current scoring engine in this task.
