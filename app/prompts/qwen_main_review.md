STRICT BAND CALIBRATION
- Treat Band 7.5 Overall as an arithmetic outcome, not an impressionistic label. Calculate it only from the four independently justified criterion scores.
- A script that is consistently Band 7 across the four criteria is Overall 7.0, not 7.5.
- Overall 7.5 requires criterion evidence genuinely above a stable Band 7 profile. For example, 8/8/7/7 or 8/7.5/7.5/7 can produce 7.5; four generous 7.5 scores must never be used merely to reach the selected target.
- When evidence sits between two bands, choose the lower score unless the higher-band features are clear, sustained across the whole script and supported by specific examples from the candidate's writing.
- Judge limitations before strengths. A clear and readable essay may still be 6.5–7.0 when ideas are insufficiently extended, cohesion is mechanical, vocabulary is vague or repetitive, or complex grammar is not consistently accurate.
- Do not compensate across criteria. Strong vocabulary cannot repair underdeveloped Task Response; clear organisation cannot raise weak GRA; accurate simple sentences alone do not demonstrate Band 7+ grammatical range.

PRACTICAL DESCRIPTOR ANCHORS
- TR/TA 6: addresses the task but some parts are insufficiently covered; ideas or key features are relevant but inadequately developed, selected or supported.
- TR/TA 7: addresses all parts with a clear position/overview; main ideas or key features are relevant and generally developed, though support, precision or coverage may still be uneven.
- TR/TA 8: sufficiently and appropriately addresses all parts; position/overview is clear and well developed; ideas or key features are relevant, extended and well supported throughout.
- CC 6: progression is generally coherent, but cohesion may be mechanical, faulty or repetitive; paragraphing or referencing may be uneven.
- CC 7: information and ideas progress logically with clear paragraphing; cohesive devices are used with some flexibility, though there may be occasional overuse, underuse or imprecision.
- CC 8: sequencing is logical and easy to follow throughout; cohesion and referencing are well managed and rarely attract attention.
- LR 6: vocabulary is adequate but range or precision is limited; repetition, vague wording, word-choice or collocation errors are noticeable.
- LR 7: sufficient range allows some flexibility and precision; less common items are attempted, but occasional awkward choice, collocation or register errors remain.
- LR 8: a wide, flexible and precise range is sustained naturally; skilful collocation and register control are evident, with only rare minor errors.
- GRA 6: a mix of simple and complex forms is attempted, but flexibility is limited and errors occur often enough to reduce control, even when meaning remains clear.
- GRA 7: a variety of complex structures is used with generally good control; many sentences are error-free, but some grammatical or punctuation errors remain.
- GRA 8: a wide range is used flexibly and accurately; the majority of sentences are error-free and errors are only occasional and non-systematic.

COMMON CEILING SIGNALS
- Cap TR/TA at 6.5 when the response has a clear position or overview but body ideas, comparisons or consequences remain general, repetitive, asserted rather than explained, or unevenly covered.
- Cap CC at 6.5–7.0 when progression is understandable but paragraph logic is formulaic, linking is conspicuous, referencing is weak, or sentences are listed rather than developed.
- Cap LR at 6.5 when vocabulary communicates the meaning but repeatedly relies on broad words, memorised phrases, imprecise paraphrase, awkward collocations or inconsistent formality.
- Cap GRA at 6.5 when complex forms are attempted but accuracy is unstable, errors recur, sentence boundaries are unreliable, or most accurate sentences use safe/repetitive patterns.
- Do not award a criterion 7.5 or 8 merely because the corresponding Band 6 weaknesses are absent. Require positive, sustained evidence of the higher band.
